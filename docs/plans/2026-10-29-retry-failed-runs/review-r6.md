1. P1 (Task 3a §§1, 3–4): stale settlement lets terminal audits change again. Two hours does not bound legitimate silence: `STEP` allows eight retries, exponential delays totalling 63.75 min, nine four-minute attempts = 99.75 min per exhausted step; a failing `persist` then a failing/retrying `fail` can be silent ~199.5 min (Cloudflare: timeout is per attempt, https://developers.cloudflare.com/workflows/build/sleeping-and-retrying/). Reconciliation settles the live workflow, frees its cap slot, allows a retry or publication; the original then resumes writing runs and "heals" its terminal audit. Sharing the `finish` lock does not stop intervening capture/persistence writes, and it defeats immutable companion inputs for terminal witnesses. Fix: a durable fence that stale settlement closes and every later workflow mutation respects (incl. `finish`); verify a resumed workflow cannot change the settled audit; correct the timing analysis; add the consecutive-failure/resumption probe. Raising the timeout alone does not establish immutability.

Evaluate stale eligibility before relaunch, or fall through after a failed relaunch with a freshly validated row; probe repeated create failures past the stale threshold.
3. P2 (Task 3a §§1, 4): heartbeat writes need a transaction and lock-order contract. Updating the audit heartbeat inside persist/fail after their writes reverses parent-before-child order (persist holds the run row and waits on the audit; settlement holds the audit and waits on the run → deadlock). Writing it separately avoids that cycle but races settlement unless the fence covers both. Specify where heartbeat and active/fence validation happen in every mutating transaction, parent locks first; add a persistence-versus-settlement probe.

Terminal potential witnesses are filtered by scenario and viewport but not structural cell identity (another page's audit enters the digest). Confirmations still fingerprint every narrow audit of the site after the floor, so a re-check of another rule invalidates publication. Narrow both to actual rule/cell dependencies using each audit's own config and `CellKey`; exercise unrelated full audits and unrelated narrow rules separately.

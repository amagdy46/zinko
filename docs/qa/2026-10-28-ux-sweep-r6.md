# UX sweep, round 6: the first-time user, end to end (2026-10-29)

`main`, read-only review. A fresh organization in `zinko_qa6` (no users at the start), the dev server that was already running against that database (port 44907; I started no process of my own), the capture engine on :8080 and the fixtures on :4400. Persona: a consultant who has just signed up and wants to send a client a report. Audited site: `meta-before-consent` (3 pages, no interaction, accept, reject, desktop, 9 runs). Method: every step below driven in a real browser at 1440x900 and read as screenshots; the mechanical checks of round 5 re-run (horizontal scroll, targets under 32px, focus rings, grayscale) at 390x844 and 1440x900, light, and dark through the account menu (Theme, Dark).

## Screens

Landing, sign up, onboarding (create organization), the empty Clients page, New client, the empty client, Add site, the empty site, New audit (defaults, filled, preflight refusal of a 404 start page), the running audit (Runs matrix, 6 minutes), the finished audit's Runs, Overview (with "2 caveats" open), Findings and the finding detail (with evidence and Explanation), the site history, Report (consultant view), the Share dialog, a created share link, the public `/r/<token>` page, Settings Branding, AI and Members, the mobile menu and the account menu (Theme).

## Result

- **Click count to a first finding:** sign up (1 form) -> organization (1 form) -> New client (2 clicks + name) -> Add site (2 clicks + 2 fields) -> New audit (1) -> edit Start URL and Key pages -> Start (1) -> wait for the audit -> Overview -> Open finding. 8 pages, no dead ends; every empty state names the next button. The path is clear; the friction is in the three places named below (the site address path, the wait, and the amber caveats on the first result).
- **Horizontal page scroll:** none on 12 routes at 1440 and 390 in light, and none on 10 routes at 390 and 1440 in dark. Tables scroll inside their container (Clients at 390).
- **Targets under 32px** (visible, outside running text): the checkboxes and radios on New audit, AI and Branding are 16x16 (their labels are the real target); the name link in the Clients, Client and Site rows is 16px high (the row is taller, the link is not); the "AI" Settings tab is 24px wide; the finding link in the report is 24px high. Nothing else.
- **Focus:** 28 Tab presses on Clients, Overview, Report and Branding landed on a ring each time (the only "no ring" stops were the body between wrap-arounds). Route changes put a visible ring on the page heading (see F17).
- **Grayscale (Overview, dark):** Critical, Partial, Not fully checked and Could not check read by glyph and word.
- **Console:** a hydration mismatch error on Settings > AI and Settings > Branding (a checkbox's `aria-hidden`), and `favicon.ico` 404 on every page. Nothing else.
- **Public report:** `/r/<token>` loads with no app chrome, "Prepared by Reyes Digital · Version 1", Save as PDF; the structure is clear (summary, scope, consent table, finding, action plan, appendix).

## Issues

Ranked by impact on a first-time user.

| Id | Severity | Issue | Suggested fix |

| F1 | Major | The first run of my first audit sat on "Running" for 3 min 46 s with no explanation: the engine answered 429 (busy), the estimate grew from "about 1 minute" to "about 10 minutes left", and the run then failed as `EngineBusy`. The audit ended **Partial**, score "Not fully checked", and the only remedy is "re-run the audit" (Re-run is disabled while it runs, then re-runs all 9). There is no cancel. A first impression of "Partial / Not fully checked" on a perfectly good site is the worst outcome of the whole journey. (The engine was busy because something else was using it; the app's behaviour is what matters.) | While the engine answers 429, say "Waiting for a capture slot" (the progress line already has that state for admission) and keep elapsed time still; retry the run a few times with a short back-off and cap the wait well under 4 minutes; retry `EngineBusy` runs at the end of the audit before settling Partial; do not let `remainingTime` grow while no run has finished; offer "Retry failed runs" (a re-check of those cells) next to the "Zinko was busy" row. Look at the capture step and retry policy in `apps/web/src/workflows`, `remainingTime` in `lib`, and the failure box on the Runs tab. |

## Top 5 to fix next

1. **F1** A busy engine must not turn the first audit into "Partial": show the wait, retry, and cap it; no growing estimate.
2. **F4** The default "answer tracking requests locally" gives every first audit an unexplained amber caveat and a permanent "could not check"; say it, or turn it off for real sites.
3. **F8** The client report prints "Zinko was busy" and "Tracking requests succeed ... says nothing either way"; client-safe wording for failure reasons and uncheckable rules.
4. **F2** The finding detail is blank "Loading" for seconds and fetches everything three times; skeleton and a single fetch on the page the product exists for.
5. **F6 with F7** The Report tab buries the document under 520px of chrome, and the share dialog cannot open the link it just made; trim the chrome and add "Open".

Everything not listed (sign up, onboarding, the empty states, Add site, the preflight error, the score block, the finding text, the Share dialog's sentences, the dark theme, 390px layouts) matched its expected state. No horizontal scroll and no missing focus ring was found.

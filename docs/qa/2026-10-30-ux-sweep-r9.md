## Method

Chromium through Playwright, 1440x900 and 390x844, light and dark (system colour scheme; round 8 already exercised the real theme menu). Beyond looking: measured `scrollWidth` on 21 routes x 4 combinations (no horizontal page scroll anywhere); measured h1 size and content column per route; computed text contrast for every text node on five routes in both themes; pressed Tab through the Sites page, the Overview and the Findings page logging each stop, its size and whether a ring shows; applied `filter: grayscale(1)` to Runs, Overview, Findings and Sites; printed the public `/r/` page to PDF (A4, print media, 11 pages) and read every page; read computed `transition` on rows, buttons and nav links.

## Result in one paragraph

The product is already well above "default shadcn": the violet-tinted neutrals, the glyph-plus-word status vocabulary, the dark theme and the Findings split view are coherent and the grayscale check passes on shape. What keeps it from looking like Linear or Vercel is not any one screen but four systemic things: (1) no consistent page frame (three different content widths, left-aligned with a dead strip on the right, titles that start at y=24 on some pages and y=56 on others); (2) one text size and weight (13px regular) does most of the work, so tables, cards and prose have no scanning hierarchy; (3) flat surfaces and weak affordances (no row hover cue, no pointer cue, ghost buttons everywhere, no score visual beyond a number); (4) the report and print output, which is the product's deliverable, has real layout bugs (a numbered list that starts at 2, half-empty pages, split finding cards). Two defects are plain bugs: the root 404 is unstyled text, and relative times can fail hydration.

## Passes (kept short)

- **Contrast:** no text node under 4.5:1 in light on Sites, Overview, Findings, Hits, Settings AI. Dark flagged three or four items per page (Finished badge, Re-run, Critical, Fail), all translucent badge fills my script read as solid; by eye they are fine (`ux9-findings-A-1440-dark.png`). Measured, not fully proven for dark.
- **Grayscale:** Critical diamond, Warning triangle, Info dot, Fail ring and the dashed Could not check ring stay distinguishable (`ux9-gray-findings.png`, `ux9-gray-matrix.png`). One weakness, V20.
- **Keyboard Tab sweep:** every stop has a ring (box-shadow ring on buttons and links, inset ring in the tab bar, outline on the two breadcrumb links), targets are 32 to 40px, order follows the visual order, a "Skip to content" link is the first stop. Two nits, V28.
- **Motion:** rows, nav links and buttons carry `transition 0.15s`; `docs/ui.md` says reduced motion stops spinners (not tested here). Nothing animates on page change or when a sheet opens beyond the library default.
- **Mobile 390:** no horizontal page scroll; Hits collapses to Vendor and Event with the run under the event (good, `ux9-hits-A-390-light.png`), the Tags table scrolls inside its card, the drawer works.
- **Auth first impression** is calm and on brand (mark, one line of value copy, card, soft gradient); see V22 for what is missing.

## Product designer notes

- **What looks most like "a debugging tool for people who trust it":** the Findings split with a 2px severity rule, the Hits table in mono, the compare wording. Keep that voice; the fixes above mostly add hierarchy, not decoration.
- **What looks like default shadcn:** the ghost and outline buttons (every secondary action is the same grey rectangle: Re-run, Share report, Open in Hits, Copy, Details), the card (white rectangle, 1px border, no depth), the plain table header, the select chevrons and the checkbox. The brand shows up only in the violet primary and the status colours.
- **Colour:** restrained and correct; the violet primary at 0.52 L in light is slightly heavy against the otherwise quiet UI, and the violet accent tint on selected rows (`bg-accent`) reads lilac-grey, which is consistent. Dark mode is the better of the two themes: surfaces separate, badges are clear.
- **Motion:** correct but absent. There is no transition between tabs (content swaps), none when the sheet opens, none when a finding is selected, and the live "Running" bar does not move. Motion should be 150 ms fades on pane and sheet content and a progress fill transition; nothing decorative.
- **Charts and score figures:** there are none beyond the number and the area bars. A score-over-time sparkline on the Site page (design review item 24, deferred) and the band bar (V10) are the two additions that would make the product feel like Plausible or Vercel analytics.
- **Print and `/r/`:** the cover, rules table with repeating header, and the legend are good print design. Fix V2, V4, V5 and it will look professional enough to hand to a client; the public page at 1440 is a comfortable 745px column and passes.

## Design system: five systemic changes

1. **One page frame.** `PageShell` (width, padding, breadcrumb row, `PageHeader` slot) replacing `PageContainer`, the audit workspace's own padding and the report's. Fixes V3, V15, V16, V29 and removes the title that moves between pages. Combine with one header component for the audit (V12).
2. **A real type ramp in three roles.** Display (`text-5xl` score, `text-2xl` page title), section (`text-lg font-semibold tracking-tight` in `SectionHeading`), label (`text-2xs font-medium uppercase tracking-wide text-muted-foreground` for table heads, card kickers and form group labels). Body stays 13px, long prose 14px at `leading-7`. Fixes V6, V7, V13, V27 in three components (`SectionHeading`, `TableHead`, `CardTitle`).
3. **Surface depth tokens.** `--card` plus a single `shadow-xs` and a stronger `--border-subtle`, the sidebar one step darker than the page, a `--surface-raised` for popovers. One token pass in `styles.css` and `components/ui/card.tsx` (V21, V23); also make disabled buttons a neutral fill instead of faded primary (V25).
4. **Interactive row primitive.** `LinkRow`/`DataRow` (hover fill, pointer, trailing chevron, focus ring inset, selected state with a 2px primary rule) used by Sites, site history, Clients, the run grid cells, the Findings list and the palette. Replaces five hand-built variants and fixes V13, V18, V19 once.
5. **A `ScoreChip` plus band bar and a `Stat` tile.** A single small score visual (number, 3px band bar, coverage qualifier) used on Sites, the site history, Clients, the Overview and the report; one `Stat` tile with fixed height. This is the product's signature number and today it is drawn four different ways (V10, V14, V30). Add skeletons that match these shapes (V26).

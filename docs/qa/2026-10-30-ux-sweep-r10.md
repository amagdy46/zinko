# UX sweep round 10: regression and integration walk (2026-10-30)

`main` at `54dc6b7` (UX rounds 8 and 9, nine batches: Q, R, S, T, U, V, W, leftovers and the r8/r9 QA notes), read-only review from worktree `zinko.qa-ux-r10`. A fresh database (`zinko_qa10`), my own dev server (`pnpm dev --stage dev_r10`, port 1337), the shared engine on :8080 and fixtures on :4400, none restarted. The `drift` fixture was moved to release 1 and back to 0 with `POST /_release/drift?to=n` (left at 0).

Each batch was verified alone; this round walks the combined result in a real browser (Playwright, Chromium, persistent profiles) with two personas:

- **Persona 1, site owner** (grouping off): "Sam Owner", organization "Sam's Shop", site "Sam's store" (`http://localhost:4400/consent-default-late/` pasted with a path). Audits: A1 `/consent-default-late/` with two key pages, all four scenarios (12 runs); A2 `/drift/` at release 0; A3 a re-run of A2 at release 1; A4 a re-check of "Personal data sent to tracking vendors" on A3. One accepted risk (mouse, then one by keyboard), one share link, a PDF of the shared report and of the draft.
- **Persona 2, agency**: the same owner turns on grouping; clients Acme and Globex, a refused duplicate ("acme "), a namesake site in each client, moves between clients and to no client; a member ("Mia Member") invited, the invite opened signed out, signed up and accepted; the member's view; a second organization.

Screenshots are `$TMPDIR/ux10-*.png` (`~/.cache/tmp/`, not committed). Widths 1440 and 390, light and dark through the real account menu Theme items; every route below was loaded in all four combinations.

## Result

- **No Critical or Major issue.** Both personas finish every step. 1 Medium, 15 Minor (16 issues, list below). Eight of the 16 are regressions or loose ends of today's batches (marked **Regression**).
- **Console, hydration, network:** a page hook collected `console.error`/`warning`, `pageerror` and every HTTP status >= 400 over the whole walk (about 130 page loads, both widths, both themes, signed in and out, `/r/`). The only events were my two deliberate 404 probes (`/app/nonexistent`, a random audit id). No hydration warning, no failed request, no dev-server error line.
- **Horizontal scroll:** none on 18 routes at 390 and at 1440 in each theme (`scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth`; tables, the tab bar and the Hits and Tags grids scroll inside their container). The signed-out `/r/` page: none at 1440 and 390.
- **Focus:** keyboard-only sign-in, sidebar, Add site dialog (focus returns to Add site on Esc), the Findings tabs, filter chips, list rows, triage menu (arrow keys), the Accept risk dialog (reason, Tab, Enter) all show a ring (computed outline or box-shadow on every stop, 14 stops on Findings, 10 on Sites, 6 on sign-in) and no keyboard trap. Sizes below 32 px: R7.
- **The redesign holds up.** The score card, Start with, Next steps (hide and "Show them" persists across reload), Findings rows and detail, Runs grid and legend, Compare, Share dialog (the new "1 active link" wording at 390), re-check disclosure, archive with Undo, rename, Archived view, password reset and change, org switcher (keeps the Settings section), invite preview, member view and the server refusal all behave as the notes say.
- **Not run:** a real LLM explanation (no key; the "switched off" callout and its "Set up AI explanations" link were seen), a grayscale pass, Safari and Windows, the 2 minute shared engine queue.

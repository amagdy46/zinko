# UX sweep, round 8: first-time site owner and agency (2026-10-30)

`main` at `5a6fd2a` (sites first, clients optional; `@zinko/decoders` rename), read-only review from worktree `zinko.qa-ux-r8`. A fresh database (`zinko_qa8`), my own dev server (`pnpm dev --stage dev_r8`, port 1337), the shared engine on :8080 and fixtures on :4400, none restarted. The `drift` fixture was moved with `POST /_release/drift?to=1` between two audits and set back to `?to=0` (verified, release 0). Method as rounds 6 and 7: driven in a real Chromium (Playwright), 1440x900 and 390x844, dark and light, the theme set through the account menu (Theme).

Two first-time users:

- **Persona 1, a site owner** (grouping off): "Sam Owner", organization "Sam's Shop", site "Sam's store" (`http://localhost:4400`). Audits: `/consent-default-late/` with two key pages and all four scenarios (12 runs), `/drift/` twice (4 runs each, release 0 then 1), one re-check of one finding. Accepted one risk with a reason, edited the report summary, published and opened the `/r/<token>` link in a signed-out context, downloaded the CSV, archived and deleted sites, signed out and in.
- **Persona 2, an agency**: the same owner turns on Settings > Organization > Group sites by client; two clients both named "Acme"; a site moved to a client and back to no client; the Clients empty state with an ungrouped site; a plain member ("Mia Member", invited through the shown link, signed up from the invite page); a second organization and the switcher.

Screenshots are in `~/.cache/tmp/ux8-*.png` (the ones cited below; the rest are the same pages in the other theme or width).

## Screens

Landing, sign up, sign in (wrong password), onboarding, empty Sites, Add site, empty site, New audit (with errors, with the copied settings), a running audit (Runs, Overview), the finished Overview, Findings (triage menu, accept-risk dialog, detail, evidence), Hits, Tag matrix, Compare (first audit, then after a drift release), Report (edit summary, Share dialog, link created, public `/r/`), re-check dialog and result, site history, site menu (Edit address, Other hosts, Archive, Delete), the Cmd+K palette, Settings (Profile, Organization, Members, AI, Branding), Clients (empty with sites, list, empty client), Change client, Invite and accept-invite, the member's Settings and Clients, the organization switcher.

## Result

- **Smooth path:** sign up, organization, site, audit, Overview, Findings, Report, share link and the signed-out `/r/` page took about 12 screens with no dead end. The round 7 fixes are visible: the site history now shows the start path under the date, the Findings coverage line is one sentence with Details, "Re-run is available when the audit has finished" is shown, and the client voice is gone from the app views.
- **Compare after a release is excellent.** After `drift` moved from 0 to 1 the Overview said "Fixed, verified by this re-run: Ad pixel fired after the visitor rejected (may be a flaky result ...); Ad pixel fired without consent. TikTok ZINKOTT0001 no longer fires ... Score 40 to 79 (+39)", and the Compare tab listed 2 Fixed (one "Low confidence"), 2 Unchanged and the stated limit about country. The wording is the best in the product.
- **Horizontal page scroll:** none at 1440 and 390 on 24 routes in light, and none on 8 routes at 390 in dark (`scrollWidth` 375 or 390 against 390, the 15px difference is the vertical scrollbar). Tables and the tab bar scroll inside their container.
- **Console and server log:** no errors or warnings except my own `/audits/<id>/runs` 404 (the Runs tab is the index route, `/runs` is a 404 page) and the expected 401 of a wrong password. Chrome logs one verbose "Input elements should have autocomplete attributes" on Settings AI.
- **Grayscale and focus** were not rerun (unchanged since round 7); not run this round.
- **Not run:** a real LLM explanation (no key; the "AI explanations are switched off" notice was seen, see H21), keyboard Tab sweep, Windows or Safari, a 2 minute wait on the shared queue (the audit queue was never full: at most 3 audits of mine at once, two ran in parallel and the third showed "Waiting for a capture slot. It starts automatically when one frees up.").

## Product designer notes

- **Hierarchy and density.** The strongest screens are the ones with one decision on them: Compare, the share dialog, the accept-risk dialog, the empty Sites page. The weakest are the audit header (H19), the Findings two-pane (H17) and the Report toolbar (H23): each carries four to seven equal-weight controls, and each is different from its neighbour tab. Compared with Linear or Vercel, where one header persists across tabs and the primary action is the only filled button, Zinko has several competing controls on the audit screens (the Report has a filled Share, three outlined buttons, a toggle and a link in one row).
- **First-time understanding.** What a first-time user does not understand: "Other hosts" (the menu item for the subdomain feature has a dense paragraph in its dialog, no example of when to use it, H3), "scenarios" versus "viewport" (the "?" help exists, good), "Could not check" in the same breath as "Critical" in the headline counts, and why a score does not rise after Accepting a risk (H20). The empty states are good, consistent and honest (each has the three-step list and a sample report), but they are the only place the product explains itself, and they disappear after the first site (the Clients empty state ignores existing sites, H8; the archived state, H1).
- **Feels unfinished versus polished SaaS.** One-way archive (H1), no rename (H3), no password reset (H26), a form that overwrites typing (H2), duplicate names allowed (H9), toasts missing on create site, archive and delete-failed-with-dialog-closed (only "Client saved" and "Moved to Accepted risk" gave feedback) and an "Audit not found" page whose only button is "Go to the app", not back to the site.
- **Where the product is ahead of the category.** Evidence behind every finding with a "Fail" row per run, the honest "Could not check", the Compare language ("Fixed, verified by this re-run", "Low confidence"), the Share dialog's plain statement of what the reader will and will not see, and the report's mobile layout.

## Persona 2 summary (agency)

Grouping on works: Clients appears next to Sites, the site menu gains "Change client", a site moves to a client and back to "No client" with a toast "Client saved" and the breadcrumb changes. A plain member sees Clients and Sites, can open audits, cannot invite (no Invite button), sees the Organization setting as read-only text ("Only owners and admins can change this"), AI as "Ask an owner or admin ...", Branding as "Only owners and admins can change branding. You can still edit and share reports." These notes are accurate and calm. Problems are H8, H9, H10, H24, H25 above.

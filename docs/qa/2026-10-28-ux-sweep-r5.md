# UX sweep, round 5: compare, re-check and the empty and partial states (2026-10-28)

`m6/core` after M6 Task 12, in the real dev server against `zinko_m6`, an organization with drift audits D0 to D2, re-checks (narrow audits), a flaky-meta chain, scratch audits in every state (queued, running with no engine taking it, partial with three runs without results, failed before start with 12 failed runs and no results, not evaluated, mixed rules versions with a null `check_title`) and an empty client and a site without audits. Method: every screen below at 390x844 and 1440x900 in the light theme (set through the account menu, Theme) and again in the dark theme (set through the real menu at 390px, where the sidebar sits behind Open menu), with an automated check per screen (horizontal page scroll, interactive elements under 32px) and the pages read as screenshots; keyboard and grayscale checks on the new controls.

## Screens

Clients, a client without sites, a client with an empty site, a site without audits, the site history (Verification badge, Compare links), the Overview of a queued, a running, a partial and a failed audit, their Compare tabs, the Findings tab of a failed audit and of the mixed-versions audit, the first audit's Compare, D2's Compare (with the sheet open), the Overview with the briefing, Findings with change chips, a re-check's Overview, Runs list, Compare and Report, the report with "What changed" in the consultant and the client view.

## Result

- **No horizontal page scroll** on any of the 27 screens at 390 and at 1440, light and dark (19 screens re-run in dark at both widths).
- **No error or warning in the console** during the sweep (the existing relative-time hydration text on the site page is a pre-existing info-level mismatch).
- **Targets under 32px:** only in the M5 report's own finding rows (the finding disclosure buttons, 16 to 24px), which this round does not change; none in the new screens.
- **Keyboard:** the Re-check dialog opens with Enter on its button, focus stays inside (Re-check, Close, Cancel, Re-check), Escape returns focus to "Re-check these pages"; the Re-run dialog returns focus to Re-run; the "Include what changed" checkbox has a visible ring and toggles with Space; the Compare rows, the six count buttons, the picker and the Not comparable trigger are reached by Tab with a visible ring (Task 7).
- **Grayscale:** the six change states, Low confidence, the notices and the briefing read by glyph and word in grayscale in both themes.
- **Dark theme** was not re-run on the M5 report in round 4; the report paper stays light by design and the new "What changed" section follows it.

## Report as a premium deliverable (batch N)

1. A real cover: logo or name once, "Tracking & consent audit", client, site, date, optionally the band as a large figure; no repeated "Prepared by", no scope box.
2. Agency summary first (N3).
3. Scoring maths only in the appendix.
4. Honest counts and wording (N1, N2).
5. The action plan as a numbered to-do list by priority, grouped by owner, with what to ask for, the severity chip and "Done when" (e.g. "Re-run: the pixel no longer fires before the choice"), ending "We recommend a re-run after fixes".
6. Typographic hierarchy: semibold labels for Why and Fix, ~65ch measure, consistent section sizes, tighter table and note line-height.
7. Plain page names (N8).
8. A scannable consent table (N14).
9. Page breaks: let the action plan share a page with the last finding; fix narrow, hyphen-broken first columns in the rules table.
10. Notes attributed: "Note from {agency}:"; on mobile, score and verdict before the cover box.

## Accessibility

Share focus and announcement (N5); public page scrollers and link rings (N7); member radios (N9); duplicate "Turn off" names (N11); logo rejection focus (N13); two h1 on the app Report tab (the paper's client name should be an h2 in the app, h1 on the public page); a `header` landmark on the public page; ~420px of toolbar before the document at 390 (collapse secondary controls into a menu).

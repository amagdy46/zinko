# UX sweep, round 7: what shipped since round 6 (2026-10-29)

`main` at `90d458f`, read-only review. A fresh user and organization in `zinko_qa6` ("Rivera Tag Studio", client "Harbor Coffee", site `http://localhost:4400`), my own dev server from the main checkout (`pnpm dev --stage dev_r7`, port 1338; `alchemy dev` only accepts a stage named `dev` or `dev_*` when the database name does not contain it), the shared engine on :8080 and fixtures on :4400, none restarted. Persona: a consultant who has just signed up and wants to send a client a report. Audited: `meta-before-consent` (3 runs, audit A) and `tiktok-after-reject` (3 runs, audit B), plus two more of the same to make one wait. Method as round 6: driven in a real browser, mechanical checks at 390x844 and 1440x900, dark and light (set through the account menu, Theme).

## Screens

Empty Clients (and its "See a sample report"), `/app/sample-report`, empty client, Add site (hint), empty site, New audit, a running audit, an audit "Waiting for a capture slot", the finished audit's Runs, Overview with "Next steps" (open, after triage, after a share link), Findings with "Fix first" rows and detail, the Report (consultant and client view), the Share dialog (first link, and reopened), the public `/r/<token>`, Settings Branding and AI.

## Result

- **Smooth path:** sign up, organization, client, site, audit, results, share link in 8 pages and no dead ends. Both new empty states offer "See a sample report" next to the primary button, and it opens a believable report inside the app shell with a "fictional data" banner. Overview "Next steps" leads from a finished audit to the share dialog in 3 clicks. The deep link `?share=1` opens the dialog on the Report tab.
- **Waiting for a capture slot (round 6 F1):** with two audits running, the third showed "Queued", "0s elapsed", "Waiting for a capture slot", 0 running, then started by itself within about 40 s and finished Finished 3 of 3. No stall, no growing estimate, no Partial. It works; what the user is told is thin (G1).
- **Next steps card:** step 1 turned "(done)" after Mark fixed; step 3 and the whole card vanished after a share link (verified: `/Next steps/` absent). Step 2 never turns done by previewing (G4).
- **Round 6 items re-checked:** see the table below. All landed except the Findings coverage line (F5) which is still dense and contradictory (G3).
- **Horizontal page scroll:** none on 16 routes at 1440 and 390 in dark, and none on 9 routes at both widths in light. Tables and the tab bar scroll inside their container.
- **Targets under 32px:** none except the 16px checkboxes and radios on New audit, Settings AI and Branding, whose label or 34px hit area is the real target (as accepted in round 6). The report's inline links are text.
- **Focus:** 30 Tab presses each on Overview, Findings, Clients and the sample report: a ring on every stop; the only "no ring" stops were the body at the wrap-around.
- **Grayscale (`filter: grayscale(1)`, site history):** Critical (diamond), Could not check (dashed ring), Finished (check ring) read by glyph and word.
- **Console:** no errors or warnings on the pages of this session.
- **Client report read as the client:** structure and honesty are good (the not-tested block is specific to the audit and names the mobile, GPC and journey gaps). The wording repeats itself and, on a finding marked fixed, contradicts itself (G2, G5, G6).

# Zinko UI

## Direction

Zinko should feel like a professional debugging tool: dense, calm and fast, in the spirit of Linear and Sentry, not a marketing dashboard. Site owners and consultants live in it, and the people they send it to see only the polished report.

- Dark and light themes, with light as the default for reports.
- Logo-tinted neutrals (hue 285) with violet `primary` as the only brand accent; `sidebar` supplies the navigation surface. Severity uses `sev-critical` (red), `sev-warning` (amber), `sev-info` (blue); passes use `status-pass` (green). Each set has plain text/glyph, `-bg`, `-border` and `-solid` tokens; status uses the same four-token set (`status-pass`, `status-active` for running, `status-neutral` for finished, queued, not audited and unknown) so a badge is `border-<set>-border bg-<set>-bg text-<set>` in both themes. Tints belong in badges, headers and detail views; rows stay neutral. `destructive` is for destructive actions and form errors.
- System sans (`ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`); no Inter download or font dependency. System monospace for IDs, parameters, URLs and timestamps. The type scale is `text-2xs` 11/16, `text-xs` 12/16, `text-sm` 13/20, `text-base` 14/22, `text-lg` 16/24, `text-xl` 20/28 and `text-2xl` 28/34 (px size/line height). Page titles are 20px (28px from `sm`). Base radius is 8px (sm 4, md 6, lg 8, xl 12).
- Meaning uses a glyph and a word: Critical a filled diamond, Warning a filled triangle, Info a filled dot; Fail an x ring, Pass a green check ring, a finished audit a neutral check ring, Partial a half ring, Could not check a neutral dashed ring, Not applicable a dash ring, Queued a hollow ring, Running a violet spinning arc. Could not check is neutral, never amber or green. Reduced motion stops spinning and pulsing indicators.
- Keyboard-first: `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` command palette (built); `j`/`k` through findings and `e` for the evidence panel are not built.
- Every number links to the evidence behind it.
- Every `/app` page sits in `PageShell` (`components/page-shell.tsx`): it owns the padding (`main` has none), the vertical rhythm and one of two widths, `full` (the default, capped at 100rem, for lists, tables and the audit workspace) and `readable` (`max-w-5xl` of content, for forms and settings; left-aligned, so every page starts at the same edge). `PageHeader` always reserves a 20px breadcrumb row, so a title is at the same height (56px from the top at 1440) with or without a breadcrumb. A shell inside a shell only keeps its spacing, so a tab or a skeleton never doubles the frame. The one exception is the compact toolbar header of the audit work tabs (Runs, Findings, Tags, Hits, Compare), which stays a single row to leave room for the work. Status and severity are never colour alone: a glyph (`StatusGlyph`) and a word sit together, so a grayscale screenshot still tells Fail, Could not check and Pass apart.
- Touch targets are at least 32px (buttons `h-8`, tabs `h-8`, icon buttons `size-8`; dense table rows are 28px with a pointer and 36px below `md`). Every interactive element shows a solid focus ring on keyboard focus, including focusable tab panels: `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-background` (`--ring` is `oklch(0.52 0.19 286)` light and `oklch(0.68 0.16 286)` dark: 4.75 to 5.95:1 on page, card, sidebar, accent, muted and popover in light and 5.02 to 6.56:1 in dark; the offset gap in the page colour keeps it visible on a primary button, 5.45:1 light and 3.87:1 dark against the fill). Where an ancestor clips overflow (scrolling tab bars, table cells, list rows, popovers) the ring is `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring focus-visible:ring-inset`. Active and selected states are never a ring: tabs underline (`tabLink` in `lib/row-style.ts`, shared by the audit and settings tab bars), the sidebar item a fill and a 2px left rule, rows a fill and a 2px primary rule on the leading edge (finding rows too: their severity is the glyph, not a coloured rule). Colours come from tokens only; the exceptions are the logo mark (`#8b7cf6`) and the `bg-black/50` scrim of dialogs.

## Design principles

1. **Lead with one number and one sentence.** A screen opens with the answer (a score, a count, a state) and one line that says what it means.
2. **A score never claims more than was checked.** Below full coverage the number says so; a bare "100/100" without full coverage is a bug.
3. **Three type roles do the work.** Display: the page title (`text-xl sm:text-2xl font-semibold tracking-tight`, `PageHeader`) and the answer (`text-5xl font-semibold tabular-nums tracking-tight`, the score). Section: `type-section` (`text-lg font-semibold tracking-tight`), applied by `SectionHeading`, `EmptyState` and, one step smaller, `CardTitle`. Label: `type-label` (`text-2xs font-medium uppercase tracking-wide text-muted-foreground`), applied by `TableHead`, the evidence and tag-matrix kickers and the small group headings. `type-section` and `type-label` are utilities in `styles.css`; do not hand-build either. Body stays `text-sm` (13px); long prose is `text-base`.
4. **Surface depth is the hierarchy.** Page (`background`), card (`card` with `border-border-subtle` and `shadow-xs`, both tokens in `styles.css`) and raised surfaces (popover, menu, select, dialog, sheet: `shadow-raised` plus a faint ring) are three levels; the sidebar is a step darker than the page in light mode and a step darker in dark. `report-paper` redeclares the light tokens, so the report stays light. A disabled solid button is a neutral fill with muted text and an inset ring, never a faded primary. Avatars have a tinted fill and a faint ring in both themes (the organization's is square). Alerts are inline rows unless they block.
5. **Explain on demand.** One sentence is visible; detail sits in a popover or a disclosure.
6. **Colour is a signal, not decoration.** One primary action per screen. The only gradient is the auth backdrop, built from tokens.
7. **Tables adapt, they do not clip.** They scroll inside their container with scroll edges, or drop columns, never cut a value off.
8. **One vocabulary, one component; an unknown status fails loudly.** A status is a typed union (`AuditStatus`, `RunStatus` in `@repo/core`); `StatusBadge` takes a `BadgeStatus`, so an unmapped value is a type error, and a stored value the app does not know is passed through `toBadgeStatus` and reads "Unknown" in a neutral badge, never the raw string.

## Shared list pieces

- **Rows that open something.** `LinkRow` (a table row) and `DataRow` (a list item, for the stacked layout below `md`) in `components/data-row.tsx`: pointer, a `bg-muted` hover and keyboard fill, `selected` as a fill plus a 2px primary rule on the leading edge, and a `RowChevron` in the last cell. The link inside carries `rowLink`, which stretches it over the row and draws the focus ring around the whole row, inset. Another control in the row (Re-run, Compare) is `relative` so it sits above the stretched link. Sites, the site history, Clients, the client's sites and the palette (`CommandItem` takes the same selected rule) use it; the Findings list, the run grid and Hits keep their own rows until a later batch moves them (`selectableRow` and `selectedRule` in `lib/row-style.ts` are the shared classes).
- **Score.** `ScoreChip` (in `score-figure.tsx`) is the score in lists: band glyph, the number, a 3px bar that is the score on a 0 to 100 track in the band colour, and "Not fully checked" under it when runs are missing. It reads `scoreCoverage`, `tooLittleCaptured` and the band the server computed with `healthScore`; it has no thresholds of its own. When there is nothing to say it shows "—" (with a screen-reader reason), never 0. `ScoreFigure` stays for the Overview, the palette and anywhere the number needs its sentence.
- **Stat.** `Stat` (`components/stat.tsx`) is a 72px tile: a one-line label (`type-label`, truncated, never wrapped) and a figure with its unit. Wrap it in a link with `group/stat` when it opens a filtered view. The Overview's three tiles use it.
- **Empty states** are a narrow centred block (`max-w-md`), with an icon that says what is missing (Globe for sites, Users for clients, SearchX for no match), two lines, an optional "What happens next" card and the actions. They never span the page; `dashed` is only for a result that was not measured.
- **Relative times** render through `RelativeTime` (`components/relative-time.tsx`): the text may differ between the server and the first client render without replacing the tree, and refreshes when the page is live and every 30 seconds. Never put `relativeTime(...).text` in server-rendered markup without `suppressHydrationWarning`.
- **Loading.** Routes with a shaped destination have their own `pendingComponent` (`SitesSkeleton`, `OverviewSkeleton`, `FindingsSkeleton` in `components/page-skeleton.tsx`); everything else falls back to `PageSkeleton`. The router waits 300 ms before showing one.
- **Root 404.** An address outside the app shows `RootNotFound` in the auth shell; inside `/app` the same words are `NotFound`.

## Vocabulary

| Where | Words |
|---|---|
| Severity | Critical, Warning, Info |
| Check result | Fail, Could not check, Pass, Not applicable (the stored word `inconclusive` never appears) |
| Triage | Open, Accepted risk, Fixed |
| Audit | Queued, Running, Finished (neutral: it says the audit ended, not that the site is fine), Partial, Failed, Not audited |
| Run | Queued, Running, Captured (a run that loaded), Partial, Failed |
| An audit without check results | Not evaluated, never shown as clean |
| Sites and clients | Sites first. "Client" appears only when the organization has switched on **Group sites by client** (Settings > Organization): then Clients, "Client: <name>", the client column and picker. A site without one reads "No client". The hidden default client is never named, listed or linked, and nothing says "ungrouped" or "default". With grouping off no menu, title, breadcrumb, palette entry, empty state, sample report or not-found page says "client" (the switch itself and its description are the one place, for owners and admins) |
| Runs view and Tags tab | The Runs tab views are Grid (runs by scenario), List and Thumbnails; "matrix" names only the Tags tab's tag-by-scenario table, and never a Runs view |
| Hits tab counts | One count: "38 events: 12 hits, 26 dataLayer pushes" (a hit is a request, a push is a dataLayer push; the tab shows hits) |
| Who signs a report | "Company" until Group sites by client is on, then "agency"; never "agency" to a site owner |
| Change since the baseline | New, Regressed, Fixed (verified by a re-run; the author's mark stays "Marked fixed"), Unchanged, Verdict changed, Not comparable. Each is a glyph and a word; Fixed reuses the Pass colour, the rest are neutral |

## Navigation

```
Sidebar: organization switcher, Find… (⌘K), [Clients,] Sites, Settings, account menu
├── Sites                         list → site (the home of an organization that does not group by client)
│   └── Site                      history of audits, New audit
│       └── Audit                 header, then tabs Overview · Runs · Findings · Compare · Tags · Hits · Report
├── Clients                       only with Group sites by client on: list → client → its sites (home when on)
└── Settings                      Profile · Organization · Members · AI · Branding
```

`/app` sends the organization to its home: `/app/clients` when it groups by client, `/app/sites` otherwise, and `/app/clients` redirects to Sites while grouping is off. A named client's URL keeps opening with grouping off (its sites show under Sites, the breadcrumb says Sites). Below `md` the sidebar is a left `Sheet` behind a menu button in a thin top bar and closes on navigation. Each page opens with a `PageHeader` (breadcrumb, title, meta line, actions). Tab order is a "Skip to content" link (visible only on focus), the navigation, then `<main id="main">`, which the link focuses.

## Screens as built

**Shell.** The sidebar opens with the wordmark (mark and "Zinko", home link), then the organization switcher (menu: organizations with a check, Members, Create organization). Items are `h-9`; the current page has `bg-accent`, `font-medium` and a 2px primary rule on its left edge. In light mode the sidebar is a slightly darker surface than the page and cards are white with a hairline ring. The foot row is the account menu: email, Theme (Light, Dark, System, a radio group, stored per browser), Sign out. The landing page keeps its own theme toggle; the sign-in and sign-up pages follow the stored theme.

**Command palette.** `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` anywhere in `/app`, or the "Find…" row (the only way on touch). Groups: Actions (New site, which opens Sites with the Add site dialog; New client, which asks for a name, and All clients, both only with grouping on; All sites; Settings; AI settings; Members; Toggle theme), Recent audits (the newest five, of sites matching the query), Sites and, with grouping on, Clients (organization-scoped names from `searchTargets`). The placeholder and the dialog description follow the mode ("Find a site or action…" / "Find a client, site or action…"), and the palette is re-keyed by organization and grouping so a switch resets a half-typed client name, New audit (one "New audit for <site>" per matching site, only once something is typed) and Switch organization. Esc closes and focus returns to where it was.

**Sites.** The home with grouping off, and under Sites with it on: a table of Site (name, origin in mono), Client (only with grouping on; "No client" in muted text), Last audit (status, relative date, score through `ScoreFigure`) and Open findings (the same rules as Clients), a search box over the names, origins and client names, and **Add site** (name, address and, with grouping on, a Client select defaulting to No client). A new organization sees an empty state ("Add your first site", a three-step list, Add site, See a sample report); `?add=true` opens the dialog and focus returns to the button after it closes. **Group sites by client** is a checkbox with a Save button in Settings > Organization ("Sites and clients"); members read one sentence and the server refuses their writes.

**Clients.** Only with grouping on. A table (Client, Sites, Last audit with a status badge, a relative date, the score through `ScoreFigure` ("Lowest of 2 sites" over several), Open findings per severity, and "Last five scores", an inline SVG sparkline on a fixed 0 to 100 scale with a hollow dot for any audit that was not fully checked); the page is `max-w-5xl`. The figures come from each site's latest settled, evaluated audit; a running audit shows "Audit running" beside them. "No open findings" appears only at full coverage with nothing that could not be checked; otherwise "0 open · not fully checked", "Not evaluated" or "Not audited". A search box filters by name; "New client" opens a dialog. A fresh organization sees an empty state and a three-step checklist (client, site, audit) that can be dismissed.

**Client and site.** The client page lists its sites (origin in mono, last audit, open findings, audits) and has "Add site"; archive and delete sit in a "…" menu behind a confirm dialog (a client or site with audits can only be archived). The site page shows "Client: <name>" (or "No client") under its title only with grouping on, and for owners and admins a **Change client** item in its "…" menu (a dialog with a client select; the next report names the new client, published versions keep theirs); "New audit" is in the header and a history table (started, status, runs "9 of 9" with "n runs could not finish", score, findings by severity or "Not evaluated"), Newer and Older. "Add site" takes an address with or without a scheme (`https://` is added) and shows a refused address under the field. A missing client, site or audit (or one in another organization) shows the app's "not found" page with "Go to the app" (`components/not-found.tsx`). A site address typed without a scheme gets `https://`, or `http://` for localhost and private hosts. "Edit address" in the site's "…" menu changes it: free for a site without audits, and for one with audits only between http:// and https:// (the host and port stay, earlier audits keep the URLs they captured). A failed preflight on an https address says to try http://. The client's "Add site" and the clients list's "New client" appear in the header only when the page is not empty, so an empty page has one action. Score cells say how many runs they come from, and finding counts hide zero severities.

**New audit.** Three numbered sections (1 Pages, 2 Scenarios and viewports, 3 Options, `border-t pt-8`) and an `h-10` Start button: Pages (start URL, key pages with per-line errors and "n of 19"), Scenarios and viewports (checkbox cards with All and Only reject, a Desktop/Mobile toggle), Options (ad-click IDs, journey, and under Advanced, answering tracking requests locally). With journey on, a numbered list shows which pages are walked. A sticky estimate (below `lg` a one-line bar, "12 runs · about 3 minutes" and Start) shows the run count, the breakdown, the duration at 7 s per work unit, and the Start button, disabled with a reason when no scenario or viewport is chosen. Pages must be on the site's origin including its port ("localhost:4400"); another port is refused. The last audit's settings prefill with the line "Settings copied from your last audit" and a Reset to defaults button (a copied start or key page that is no longer on the site is dropped). Each option has a one-line gloss (click IDs, journey, answering tracking requests locally, Global Privacy Control) and a help popover sits beside "Consent scenarios". Start loads the start page once on the server (`preflight` in `server/audits.ts`: redirects followed by hand and kept on the site, private hosts refused outside dev) and a page that cannot be reached, answers 404, 410 or 5xx, or redirects off the site is reported under the Start URL before any audit exists. The time left on a running audit is recalculated from its own pace (`remainingTime`), one figure, never a range.

**Audit and runs.** Every tab shows the same header (round 9, V12): the breadcrumb (Sites, the site, "Audit"), the title at the `PageShell` position with Re-run and Share report on its right, then one meta line: the status, the facts ("30 Oct 2026, 02:21 · 2 pages · /checkout/"; the day only until hydration, when the reader's time zone is known), the run summary ("28 runs: 26 captured, 2 partial, 0 failed"; "28 runs" alone below `sm`), open findings by severity ("5 open: 4 Critical, 1 Warning", the same count as the Findings tab, accepted and fixed excluded), the duration and a pill "n of m runs could not finish · See why" linking to the Runs list. While the audit runs the meta line shows the status, the facts and "Live", Re-run is not shown (no disabled button repeating the progress sentence) and the progress bar sits under it. The old compact one-row header and the "See results" button are gone; the Overview tab is the way in. A run that loaded is "Captured" (neutral glyph, never a green check: Findings says pass or fail); a matrix cell shows the worst failing severity with its failing-check count, then the hits, and a captured run with 0 hits says no tracking was seen. Pages show as paths without the query, the full URL in a tooltip, and "+ click IDs" when the query carries them. On Findings (lg and up) the list and detail pane fill the viewport and scroll on their own. In the finding detail, "Where it was seen" is a list of two-line rows and empty evidence tabs are hidden; Accept risk says "A reason is required" and the toast offers Undo. The header shows the status, a live dot (Connected / Reconnecting) and either progress (a bar, "12 of 44 runs", elapsed, time left, counts of running, partial and failed) or, when finished, that summary line. While the audit runs, a box names every failed or partial run with its reason; once it finished, Overview carries the one notice (below). Tabs carry counts. A finished audit with at least one evaluated run opens on Overview (an audit with none, or one still running, opens on Runs; a `view` or `run` in the URL is kept); Runs stays one click away and the completion toast's action is "Overview". The Runs tab has three views: a matrix (page rows, scenario columns grouped by viewport, a glyph and the hit count per cell, a hover card with the screenshot), a list and thumbnails. A cell opens the run sheet (`?run=`, a right `Sheet`, a bottom one below `md`): screenshot, facts, reason, the page's other scenarios, previous and next (arrow keys), Open in Hits and the failing findings that cite the run. A toast announces completion once. Dates are "28 Oct 2026, 02:21" (`shortDateTime`) in the reader's time zone, never the US form; they render in UTC on the server and switch after hydration (`useTimeZone`). While an audit has no run on a capture engine the progress line says "Waiting for a capture slot" and elapsed time does not run until the first run starts. After a route change focus moves to the page's heading (never on first load); the sidebar marks Clients on client, site, audit and new-audit pages; the Find palette input has a focus ring and its recent audits show date, status and score; tables show a soft edge where they scroll sideways.

**Overview** (`/overview`). The score block is a card: left the number out of 100 at display size, a 6px band bar under it (`BandBar` in `score-figure.tsx`, the same bar `ScoreChip` draws at 3px; an empty track when the score is "—"), a band word and glyph (Critical issues, Needs attention, Minor issues only, No failing checks; never "healthy" or "clean") and a quiet "How this is scored" button; right three `Stat` tiles of one geometry (Critical, Warning open, Could not check). "Start with <severity> <worst open finding>" is a card of two aligned columns (Why it matters, with the first sentence in ink and the rest in muted type as separate paragraphs, and How to fix it with its copy button in a label row of the same height) and Open in Findings and Open in Hits below. "Next steps" is three numbered steps in a row (a check once done, derived as before) with a Hide button; hiding is remembered in this browser only (`localStorage` key `zinko:next-steps-hidden`, `useLocalFlag` in `lib/local-flag.ts`) and a one-line "Show them" brings the card back (the flag is one per browser, shared by every audit and organization on purpose: it is a preference about the card, not about an audit; focus moves to Show them or back to Hide, and the line is a status region); once a share link exists it is one line as before. When some findings are accepted risks the block adds "The score of 28 counts all findings, including 1 accepted risk. Without it: 42." (the same `healthScore` run on the findings without accepted risks; fixed findings still count; see Audit names and score with accepted risks). "How this is scored" opens beside the button, limited to the room Radix reports (`--radix-popover-content-available-height`, 16px collision padding) and scrolling inside, and starts with "Starts at 100. Each failing check costs points, more for Critical, more when it fails on more pages.", then the per-rule lines and the sum; a Details disclosure holds the formula, weights, the Critical cap (79), what is not scored, the score version and the rules versions. The purchase-fields caveat appears only when that rule failed. Status lines: "Partly evaluated: 38 of 40 runs have check results" only when some runs have none; "Could not check: 6 rules, on 9 runs" is a disclosure listing the check titles; "All 40 runs evaluated, every check resolved" only when neither applies. When the start page could not be loaded (every run of it failed or left with a Navigation, HttpStatus or Timeout error), when any other key page could not (the alert names it: "Could not load /nope-404/: Page returned 404 (Not Found)") or at least half the runs did not finish, a warning alert "This audit is incomplete" leads the page (it is the only notice about unfinished runs: a finished audit with partial or failed runs but a usable score gets "2 of 28 runs could not finish (2 partial)" in the same place, and the affected runs sit behind a "See which runs" disclosure) and the score reads "Incomplete" in muted type beside the band. A neutral dashed alert "n failing consent checks rest on an undetected banner" (banner detection uncertainty) is the one place that says so, with the affected rules; the findings keep their weight. On Findings the list row says "banner not confirmed on n runs" and the finding's run table marks each affected failing run "Banner not detected" or "Banner status unknown". Mixed rules versions add "mixed rules versions" beside the band and an alert. After the banner alert, the worst open failing finding is shown in full: severity, title, "failed on 16 of 36 runs where it could be checked, across 3 pages and 4 scenarios", why it matters, the fix steps with a copy button, the stored message of the first failing run, and Open finding and Open in Hits (that run). Triaging it promotes the next; with none open the section says "No open failing findings". Counts by area is a table (Critical, Warning, Info, Could not check; compact padding so all columns fit at 390px) of the areas with a failing or could-not-check rule, whose non-zero counts link to Findings with `area` and `severity`; below it "No failures in X, Y: checks ran and passed." lists areas where checks passed and "Nothing was checked in Z (not evaluated or not applicable)." the rest. Tags seen has one link per vendor ("Google Analytics 4: 2 destinations, 3 events") to the Tag matrix. Not evaluated is an `EmptyState`; a running audit says "Available when the audit finishes".

**Report** (`/report`). A status chip (a muted pill with a dot: hollow for "Draft, not shared", amber for unpublished changes, filled for "Version 1 published"; it is not a button) beside the Share button, and a Branding link (owners and admins; members read "Ask an owner or admin") above the document. The document is `ReportView`, a pure component on `.report-paper` (always light, at most 210mm wide, the agency's accent as a top band): a real cover (the logo, or the agency name when there is no logo, once; "Tracking & consent audit"; the client as the page's h1 on a shared link and an h2 inside the app; site and host; "Audited on 28 October 2026, 22:00 UTC" and, once published, "Version n, published ..."; no scope box, no "Prepared by"), alone on page 1 in print. Dates on the paper are UTC with the time: a published page is static HTML with no reader's zone, so the report states its own and the time lets a reader place it against their day. Then the summary, in this order: the agency's text ("Summary by <agency>") or, without one, "Key findings" (the generated headlines); the incomplete banner; the score as `ScoreFigure` (same honesty tiers as the Overview); counts; "How the score adds up is in Appendix D" (the scoring maths live only in the appendix); "Start with" as a block (severity, title, then where it failed: "failed on 16 of 36 runs where it could be checked, on 3 pages, in all 4 scenarios", counting only pages and scenarios with a failing run); the checks that could not run, each kind in its own line ("1 page could not be loaded (Page 2); 21 checks could not run there.", a check that could not be checked on any run named on its own line, then "n checks could not be run on m runs: " and the first three by their stored check title, "and n more" after; Appendix A has them all, model version 7); the undetected-banner note; and, when the agency wrote a summary, "Key findings" after it. Headlines and "Start with" skip findings hidden from the detail and add "n more findings count in the score but are not detailed here." Then Scope and method and Consent scenarios (vendor by scenario; a cell is a glyph and one line, "Fired · 10 of 10" or "None seen · 0 of 10", with "n before, m after the choice" only where the vendor fired in Accept all or Reject all and the choice was seen; * marks partial captures and † missing runs, explained in one footnote list).
**Report entry points.** The audit header's **Share report** opens the Report tab with the share dialog (on the Report tab itself the header omits it: the status row has Share); the Overview and Findings end with "Ready to send? Open the report". The Report tab starts with one slim strip (`ReportIntro`): "Before you share it: ..." with Dismiss (remembered in `localStorage`, `zinko:report-intro-dismissed`; not shown once a version of the report exists) and, when the organization has no logo and its agency name is still the organization's name, a line "No agency branding yet" (owners and admins get a link to Settings > Branding, members read "Ask an owner or admin to add it."). The status chip, Share and the toolbar share one row. The Consultant/Shared view is `?view=client` in the URL. The horizontal scroll areas inside the report show the focus ring.

**Report editing.** The toolbar has a Consultant view / Shared view toggle and "Show page addresses: Off" (or "On"), which opens a dialog listing exactly what On would print beyond Off (the entered paths and the Meta and TikTok IDs in the evidence; Google Analytics and Google Ads IDs print either way) before the choice is saved. They still count in the score."); the score and counts still include it. A note prints as "Note from <agency>". Text that looks like an email or phone number saves with a warning. Dialogs open from their own buttons, so focus returns to them. Below `sm` the toolbar keeps the view toggle, the chip and Share, and folds Show page addresses, Save as PDF, Export CSV and Branding into a "More" menu (Show page addresses opens its dialog from the menu and returns focus to More).

**Share dialog.** Creating a link moves focus to the link field and announces "Link created for version 1. It is shown once." (`role="status"`); the pre-flight "This publishes version n" or "unchanged since version n" line is hidden once a link is shown. ## Score honesty (batch I)

`components/score-figure.tsx` is the one place that shows a score: Overview (`hero`), Clients, client page, site history (`cell`) and the command palette (`inline`). It takes pure props (`score`, a `ScoreCoverage`, an optional band) so the report can reuse it. `scoreCoverage` in `lib/health-score.ts` sorts a score into two tiers. Tier 1, "Not fully checked" (muted number, partial glyph and words, a no-failures band reading "No failures in what was checked"): run coverage is incomplete, meaning some run has no check results or ended partial or failed (which covers an incomplete audit), or a client has a site with no audit. It states exact run counts ("1 of 2 runs checked", "2 runs did not finish", "6 sites not audited yet"). Tier 2: every run was evaluated but some checks could not be resolved. The number keeps full ink and a quiet line says "18 of 19 checks resolved"; a no-failures band reads "No failures in what was checked" with a dashed glyph, never a pass-sounding band. Tier 2 is deliberately quiet because it is near-constant: `hygiene.tag-errors` cannot tell when vendor requests are answered locally, so it is inconclusive on almost every audit, and a label on every audit would mean nothing. Full ink with the plain band and "All runs evaluated, every check resolved" only when every check resolved. The caveats disclosure still lists the unresolved rules. Banner uncertainty is a caveat on findings, not on coverage. The score formula and version are unchanged.

In the Findings coverage line, the per-run counts leave out `hygiene.tag-errors` when it is "could not check" because tracking requests are answered locally (every local audit): otherwise every run would read "could not check" and none "passed". The line says once that endpoint delivery is not checked locally and is left out of those counts; the check is still listed as a Could not check rule and is never counted as passed.

Counts have one definition each. "Could not check" is a rule whose finding has kind `inconclusive`: it could not be checked on any run it flagged and failed on none (a rule that also fails somewhere is a failing finding, not a could-not-check one). The Overview tile, the caveat (first 5 titles, then an "and n more" disclosure), the area strip, the Findings "Could not check (n)" group, the Clients table and the audits list all count that. Check results that could not run (`score.uncertainty.checks`) are a separate unit, named "checks". Findings coverage reads "36 of 40 runs evaluated; 4 did not finish": evaluated means finished with check results, and a partial or failed run is never counted as evaluated even when it has (inconclusive) check results. `ScoreFigure` shows "—" and "Not enough was captured to score" when half the runs or fewer were captured (evaluated and finished), in every size.

Layout: `PageContainer` (`max-w-5xl`, left-aligned) wraps Clients, client, site, New audit and Settings so they share the audit workspace's left edge; the audit workspace is full width. The Clients "Last audit" cell is two lines (status and time; inline score), and the mobile audit header hides the severity split, duration and "See why" to stay within two lines.

Overview top: one card with the large score and three tiles (Critical and Warning open, linking to Findings, and Could not check rules); then one "n caveats" disclosure (runs that did not finish with "See which runs", checks that could not run, undetected banner, mixed rules versions), open only when the audit is incomplete. The worst-finding prose is `max-w-prose` with its first sentence in foreground. Counts by area is one row per area with a segmented SVG bar (token fills, scaled to the busiest area) and the counts as linked text with glyphs.

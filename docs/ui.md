# Zinko UI

## Direction

Zinko should feel like a professional debugging tool: dense, calm and fast, in the spirit of Linear and Sentry, not a marketing dashboard. Site owners and consultants live in it, and the people they send it to see only the polished report.

- Dark and light themes, with light as the default for reports.
- Logo-tinted neutrals (hue 285) with violet `brand` as the only accent, for where you are and what is happening (selected row or cell, active tab, focus ring, running, link underline), never a button, a large fill, a status or the client's report. `primary` is ink: buttons, checkboxes and other actions; `sidebar` supplies the sign-in panel surface. Severity uses `sev-critical` (red), `sev-warning` (amber), `sev-info` (blue); passes use `status-pass` (green). Each set has plain text/glyph, `-bg`, `-border` and `-solid` tokens; status uses the same four-token set (`status-pass`, `status-active` for running, `status-neutral` for finished, queued, not audited and unknown) so a badge is `border-<set>-border bg-<set>-bg text-<set>` in both themes. Tints belong in badges, headers and detail views; rows stay neutral. `destructive` is for destructive actions and form errors.
- IBM Plex Sans (variable) and IBM Plex Mono, self-hosted through `@fontsource` so no request leaves the app, with the system stacks as the fallback. Plex Mono is for IDs, parameters, URLs and timestamps. The public report allows them with `font-src 'self'`. The type scale is `text-2xs` 11/16, `text-xs` 12/16, `text-sm` 13/20, `text-base` 14/22, `text-lg` 16/24, `text-xl` 20/28 and `text-2xl` 28/34 (px size/line height). Page titles are 20px (28px from `sm`). Base radius is 8px (sm 4, md 6, lg 8, xl 12).
- Meaning uses a glyph and a word: Critical a filled diamond, Warning a filled triangle, Info a filled dot; Fail an x ring, Pass a green check ring, a finished audit a neutral check ring, Partial a half ring, Could not check a neutral dashed ring, Not applicable a dash ring, Queued a hollow ring, Running a violet spinning arc. Could not check is neutral, never amber or green. Reduced motion stops spinning and pulsing indicators.
- Keyboard-first: `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` command palette (built); `j`/`k` through findings and `e` for the evidence panel are not built.
- Every number links to the evidence behind it.
- Every `/app` page sits in `PageShell` (`components/page-shell.tsx`): it owns the padding (`main` has none), the vertical rhythm and one of two widths, `full` (the default, capped at 100rem, for lists, tables and the audit workspace) and `readable` (`max-w-5xl` of content, for forms and settings; left-aligned, so every page starts at the same edge). `PageHeader` always reserves a 20px breadcrumb row, so a title is at the same height (56px from the top at 1440) with or without a breadcrumb. A shell inside a shell only keeps its spacing, so a tab or a skeleton never doubles the frame. An audit page sits under the audit header and its tab rows; a tab's own toolbar stays a single row to leave room for the work. Status and severity are never colour alone: a glyph (`StatusGlyph`) and a word sit together, so a grayscale screenshot still tells Fail, Could not check and Pass apart.
- Touch targets are at least 32px (buttons `h-8`, tabs `h-8`, icon buttons `size-8`; dense table rows are 28px with a pointer and 36px below `md`). Every interactive element shows a solid focus ring on keyboard focus, including focusable tab panels: `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-background` (`--ring` is `oklch(0.52 0.19 286)` light and `oklch(0.68 0.16 286)` dark: 4.75 to 5.95:1 on page, card, sidebar, accent, muted and popover in light and 5.02 to 6.56:1 in dark; the offset gap in the page colour keeps it visible on a primary button, 5.45:1 light and 3.87:1 dark against the fill). Where an ancestor clips overflow (scrolling tab bars, table cells, list rows, popovers) the ring is `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring focus-visible:ring-inset`. Active and selected states are never a ring: tabs underline (`tabLink` in `lib/row-style.ts`, shared by the audit and settings tab bars), the current site in the switcher a 2px left rule, rows a fill and a 2px brand rule on the leading edge (finding rows too: their severity is the glyph, not a coloured rule). Colours come from tokens only; the exceptions are the logo mark (`#8b7cf6`) and the `bg-black/50` scrim of dialogs.

## Design principles

1. **Lead with one number and one sentence.** A screen opens with the answer (a score, a count, a state) and one line that says what it means.
2. **A score never claims more than was checked.** Below full coverage the number says so; a bare "100/100" without full coverage is a bug.
3. **Three type roles do the work.** Display: the page title (`text-xl sm:text-2xl font-semibold tracking-tight`, `PageHeader`) and the answer (`text-5xl font-semibold tabular-nums tracking-tight`, the score). Section: `type-section` (`text-lg font-semibold tracking-tight`), applied by `SectionHeading`, `EmptyState` and, one step smaller, `CardTitle`. Label: `type-label` (`text-2xs font-medium uppercase tracking-wide text-muted-foreground`), applied by `TableHead`, the evidence and tag-matrix kickers and the small group headings. `type-section` and `type-label` are utilities in `styles.css`; do not hand-build either. Body stays `text-sm` (13px); long prose is `text-base`.
4. **Surface depth is the hierarchy.** Page (`background`), card (`card` with `border-border-subtle` and `shadow-xs`, both tokens in `styles.css`) and raised surfaces (popover, menu, select, dialog, sheet: `shadow-raised` plus a faint ring) are three levels. `report-paper` redeclares the light tokens, so the report stays light. A disabled solid button is a neutral fill with muted text and an inset ring, never a faded primary. Avatars have a tinted fill and a faint ring in both themes (the organization's is square). Alerts are inline rows unless they block.
5. **Explain on demand.** One sentence is visible; detail sits in a popover or a disclosure.
6. **Colour is a signal, not decoration.** One primary action per screen. The only gradient is the auth backdrop, built from tokens.
7. **Tables adapt, they do not clip.** They scroll inside their container with scroll edges, or drop columns, never cut a value off.
8. **One vocabulary, one component; an unknown status fails loudly.** A status is a typed union (`AuditStatus`, `RunStatus` in `@repo/core`); `StatusBadge` takes a `BadgeStatus`, so an unmapped value is a type error, and a stored value the app does not know is passed through `toBadgeStatus` and reads "Unknown" in a neutral badge, never the raw string.

## Shared list pieces

- **Rows that open something.** `LinkRow` (a table row) and `DataRow` (a list item, for the stacked layout below `md`) in `components/data-row.tsx`: pointer, a `bg-muted` hover and keyboard fill, `selected` as a fill plus a 2px brand rule on the leading edge, and a `RowChevron` in the last cell. The link inside carries `rowLink`, which stretches it over the row and draws the focus ring around the whole row, inset. Another control in the row (Re-run, Open runs) is `relative` so it sits above the stretched link. Sites, the site history, Clients, the client's sites and the palette (`CommandItem` takes the same selected rule) use it; the Findings list, the run grid and Hits keep their own rows until a later batch moves them (`selectableRow` and `selectedRule` in `lib/row-style.ts` are the shared classes).
- **Score.** `ScoreChip` (in `score-figure.tsx`) is the score in lists: band glyph, the number, a 3px bar that is the score on a 0 to 100 track in the band colour, and "Not fully checked" under it when runs are missing. It reads `scoreCoverage`, `tooLittleCaptured` and the band the server computed with `healthScore`; it has no thresholds of its own. When there is nothing to say it shows "—" (with a screen-reader reason), never 0. `ScoreFigure` stays for the Overview, the palette and anywhere the number needs its sentence.
- **Stat.** `Stat` (`components/stat.tsx`) is a 72px tile: a one-line label (`type-label`, truncated, never wrapped) and a figure with its unit. Wrap it in a link with `group/stat` when it opens a filtered view. The Overview's three tiles use it.
- **Empty states** are a narrow centred block (`max-w-md`), with an icon that says what is missing (Globe for sites, SearchX for no match), two lines, an optional "What happens next" card and the actions. They never span the page; `dashed` is only for a result that was not measured.
- **Relative times** render through `RelativeTime` (`components/relative-time.tsx`): the text may differ between the server and the first client render without replacing the tree, and refreshes when the page is live and every 30 seconds. Never put `relativeTime(...).text` in server-rendered markup without `suppressHydrationWarning`.
- **Loading.** Routes with a shaped destination have their own `pendingComponent` (`SitesSkeleton`, `FindingsSkeleton` in `components/page-skeleton.tsx`); everything else falls back to `PageSkeleton`. The router waits 300 ms before showing one.
- **Root 404.** An address outside the app shows `RootNotFound` in the auth shell; inside `/app` the same words are `NotFound`.

## Vocabulary

| Where | Words |
|---|---|
| Severity | Critical, Warning, Info |
| Check result | Fail, Could not check, Pass, Not applicable (the stored word `inconclusive` never appears) |
| Triage | Open, Accepted risk, Fixed |
| Audit | Queued, Running, Finished (neutral: it says the audit ended, not that the site is fine), Partial, Failed, Not audited |
| Run | Queued, Running, Captured (a run that loaded), Partial, Failed |
| Why a run failed because its audit stopped | Interrupted: "The audit stopped before this run finished" (a run of an audit whose workflow ended or went silent and was settled; never "failed" alone and never "The browser engine failed") |
| An audit without check results | Not evaluated, never shown as clean |
| Sites | The site is the only thing you organize by. No clients, groups or projects, and nothing says "ungrouped" or "default". "Report for" is the optional name a site's report is for |
| What a visitor does with the consent banner | Choice: No interaction, Accept all, Reject all, Global Privacy Control. Never "scenario" on screen; `scenario` stays in code, stored data, URLs and the CLI flag |
| Runs view and Tags tab | The Runs tab views are Grid (runs by choice), List and Thumbnails; "matrix" names only the Tags tab's tag-by-choice table, and never a Runs view |
| Hits tab counts | One count: "38 events: 12 hits, 26 dataLayer pushes" (a hit is a request, a push is a dataLayer push; the tab shows hits) |
| Who signs a report | "Company" |
| Audit tabs | Problems, Evidence (with Runs, Tags, Hits), Report (with its state: Draft, Published, Shared), and Changes at the right. On screen a failing check is a "problem"; the route stays `/findings`, the code says finding, and the report keeps its own word. |
| Change since the baseline | New, Regressed, Fixed (verified by a re-run; the author's mark stays "Marked fixed"), Unchanged, Verdict changed, Not comparable. Each is a glyph and a word; Fixed reuses the Pass colour, the rest are neutral |

## Navigation

```
Top bar: mark / organization / site (switcher) / where you are, then Find (⌘K) and the account menu (Settings, Theme, Sign out)
├── Sites                         list → site (the home of every organization)
│   └── Site                      history of audits, New audit
│       └── Audit                 the answer as the title, then tabs Problems · Evidence (Runs · Tags · Hits) · Report · Changes
└── Settings                      Profile · Members · AI · Branding
```

`/app` sends the organization to Sites. The top bar is the only navigation at every width. Each page opens with a `PageHeader` (title, meta line, actions).

## Screens as built

**Shell.** `TopBar` (`components/top-bar.tsx`) is a 56px row above the page that scrolls with it, so sticky elements and tall tables keep their room. It reads where you are from the loaded routes (`useMatch`); no page reports it. The mark opens Sites. The organization crumb is a menu (organizations with a check, Create organization) and shrinks to its initial below `sm`. The site crumb is `SiteSwitcher`: the name opens the site (or Sites when no site is in view), the chevron opens a type-ahead list of every active site with its address and score, running state or "Not audited", then All sites and Add site. The list is fetched on every opening; the current site has a 2px brand rule. The last crumb (Audit, Re-check, New audit, Archived, Sample report) is hidden below `md`. Settings and the design system show no switcher. At the right: Find with its shortcut (an icon below `md`) and the account avatar, whose menu has name and email, Settings, Theme (Light, Dark, System, a radio group, stored per browser) and Sign out. In light mode cards are white with a hairline ring. The landing page keeps its own theme toggle; the sign-in and sign-up pages follow the stored theme.

**Command palette.** `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` anywhere in `/app`, or the Find button in the top bar (the only way on touch). Groups: Actions (New site, which opens Sites with the Add site dialog; All sites; Settings; AI settings; Members; Toggle theme), Recent audits (the newest five, of sites matching the query), Sites (organization-scoped names from `searchTargets`), New audit (one "New audit for <site>" per matching site, only once something is typed) and Switch organization. The palette is re-keyed by organization so a switch resets it. Esc closes and focus returns to where it was.

**Sites.** The home: a table of Site (name, origin in mono), Last audit (status, relative date, score through `ScoreFigure`) and Open findings, a "Needs attention" list above the table (see Patterns), a search box over the names and origins, and **Add site** (name and address). A new organization sees an empty state ("Add your first site", a three-step list, Add site, See a sample report); `?add=true` opens the dialog and focus returns to the button after it closes.


**Site.** The site page shows "Report for <name>" under its title when set, and its "…" menu has Rename, Edit address, **Report for** (a dialog; the next report's cover prints it, published versions keep theirs), and for owners and admins Other hosts, Archive and Delete behind a confirm dialog (a site with audits can only be archived). "New audit" is in the header and a history table (started, status, runs "9 of 9" with "n runs could not finish", score, findings by severity or "Not evaluated"), Newer and Older. "Add site" takes an address with or without a scheme (`https://`, or `http://` for localhost and private hosts) and shows a refused address under the field. A missing site or audit (or one in another organization) shows the app's "not found" page with "Go to the app" (`components/not-found.tsx`). "Edit address" changes the address: free for a site without audits, and for one with audits only between http:// and https:// (the host and port stay, earlier audits keep the URLs they captured). A failed preflight on an https address says to try http://. Score cells say how many runs they come from, and finding counts hide zero severities.

**New audit.** Three numbered sections (1 Pages, 2 Choices and viewports, 3 Options, `border-t pt-8`) and an `h-10` Start button: Pages (start URL, key pages with per-line errors and "n of 19"), Choices and viewports (checkbox cards with All and Only reject, a Desktop/Mobile toggle), Options (ad-click IDs, journey, and under Advanced, answering tracking requests locally). With journey on, a numbered list shows which pages are walked. A sticky estimate (below `lg` a one-line bar, "12 runs · about 3 minutes" and Start) shows the run count, the breakdown, the duration at 7 s per work unit, and the Start button, disabled with a reason when no choice or viewport is chosen. Pages must be on the site's origin including its port ("localhost:4400"); another port is refused. The last audit's settings prefill with the line "Settings copied from your last audit" and a Reset to defaults button (a copied start or key page that is no longer on the site is dropped). Each option has a one-line gloss (click IDs, journey, answering tracking requests locally, Global Privacy Control) and a help popover sits beside "Consent choices". Start loads the start page once on the server (`preflight` in `server/audits.ts`: redirects followed by hand and kept on the site, private hosts refused outside dev) and a page that cannot be reached, answers 404, 410 or 5xx, or redirects off the site is reported under the Start URL before any audit exists. The time left on a running audit is recalculated from its own pace (`remainingTime`), one figure, never a range.

**Audit and runs.** Every tab shows the same header: for a full audit the title is the answer, set large: "5 problems", "No open problems" only for a complete audit with nothing that could not be checked, otherwise "No problems in what was checked", "Not evaluated", or "14 of 24 runs" while running. A re-check keeps its own title at the normal size. Then the open counts by severity (links that filter the list), the status when it is not complete, the facts ("30 Oct 2026, 02:21 · 2 pages · /checkout/"; the day only until hydration), and the pill when runs could not finish, with Re-run and Share report on the right. The stage strip is gone: the Report tab says its state and Re-run is the next step.

**Problems** (`/findings`; `/overview` redirects here and a finished audit lands here). From the top: the score (`ScoreChip`, or `ScoreFigure` with its reason when too little was captured) with "How this is scored" (the accepted-risk note, the arithmetic, the details), or for a re-check its result; the Briefing when there is a baseline; one collapsed "n caveats" row (`Caveats`, `components/audit-summary.tsx`: unfinished runs, could-not-check rules, undetected banners, mixed rules versions); then the list and the proof pane. While an audit runs the Coverage alert replaces score and caveats. The list has triage tabs, one Filter menu (severity and area, in the URL) and Export CSV; rows are title, who usually fixes it, the change chip and "n of m runs". A clean audit has no empty pane. Tags live in Evidence and the worst finding is the first selected row.

**Report** (`/report`). A status chip (a muted pill with a dot: hollow for "Draft, not shared", amber for unpublished changes, filled for "Version 1 published"; it is not a button) beside the Share button, and a Branding link (owners and admins; members read "Ask an owner or admin") above the document. The document is `ReportView`, a pure component on `.report-paper` (always light, at most 210mm wide, the agency's accent as a top band): a real cover (the logo, or the agency name when there is no logo, once; "Tracking & consent audit"; the client as the page's h1 on a shared link and an h2 inside the app; site and host; "Audited on 28 October 2026, 22:00 UTC" and, once published, "Version n, published ..."; no scope box, no "Prepared by"), alone on page 1 in print. Dates on the paper are UTC with the time: a published page is static HTML with no reader's zone, so the report states its own and the time lets a reader place it against their day. Then the summary, in this order: the agency's text ("Summary by <agency>") or, without one, "Key findings" (the generated headlines); the incomplete banner; the score as `ScoreFigure` (same honesty tiers as the Overview); counts; "How the score adds up is in Appendix D" (the scoring maths live only in the appendix); "Start with" as a block (severity, title, then where it failed: "failed on 16 of 36 runs where it could be checked, on 3 pages, in all 4 choices", counting only pages and choices with a failing run); the checks that could not run, each kind in its own line ("1 page could not be loaded (Page 2); 21 checks could not run there.", a check that could not be checked on any run named on its own line, then "n checks could not be run on m runs: " and the first three by their stored check title, "and n more" after; Appendix A has them all, model version 7); the undetected-banner note; and, when the agency wrote a summary, "Key findings" after it. Headlines and "Start with" skip findings hidden from the detail and add "n more findings count in the score but are not detailed here." Then Scope and method and Consent choices (vendor by choice; a cell is a glyph and one line, "Fired · 10 of 10" or "None seen · 0 of 10", with "n before, m after the choice" only where the vendor fired in Accept all or Reject all and the choice was seen; * marks partial captures and † missing runs, explained in one footnote list).
**Report entry points.** The audit header's **Share report** opens the Report tab with the share dialog (on the Report tab itself the header omits it: the status row has Share);  The Report tab starts with one slim strip (`ReportIntro`): "Before you share it: ..." with Dismiss (remembered in `localStorage`, `zinko:report-intro-dismissed`; not shown once a version of the report exists) and, when the organization has no logo and its agency name is still the organization's name, a line "No agency branding yet" (owners and admins get a link to Settings > Branding, members read "Ask an owner or admin to add it."). The status chip, Share and the toolbar share one row. The Consultant/Shared view is `?view=client` in the URL. The horizontal scroll areas inside the report show the focus ring.

**Report editing.** The toolbar has a Consultant view / Shared view toggle and "Show page addresses: Off" (or "On"), which opens a dialog listing exactly what On would print beyond Off (the entered paths and the Meta and TikTok IDs in the evidence; Google Analytics and Google Ads IDs print either way) before the choice is saved. Consultant-typed page names are not built: pages are "Page 1", "Page 2" until On. In Consultant view the Summary has Edit summary (a right sheet with a full-height textarea, 2000 characters, "Start from the generated summary" copies the generated sentences in to edit, empty goes back to the generated summary alone) and each finding has Add note, Change owner and "Hide detail, keep in score". A hidden finding stays in Consultant view dimmed with a "Hidden from the detail" chip and its controls ("Show detail again"), is absent from Shared view, the plan, the headlines and the report model's findings, and is listed by title under Appendix C ("<agency> chose not to detail these findings here. They still count in the score."); the score and counts still include it. A note prints as "Note from <agency>". Text that looks like an email or phone number saves with a warning. Dialogs open from their own buttons, so focus returns to them. Below `sm` the toolbar keeps the view toggle, the chip and Share, and folds Show page addresses, Save as PDF, Export CSV and Branding into a "More" menu (Show page addresses opens its dialog from the menu and returns focus to More).

**Share dialog.** Creating a link moves focus to the link field and announces "Link created for version 1. It is shown once." (`role="status"`); the pre-flight "This publishes version n" or "unchanged since version n" line is hidden once a link is shown. Under Link expires a line gives the actual date ("Stops working on 27 Nov 2026.") or "Works until you turn it off." for Never, and an incomplete audit adds "This audit is incomplete. The report says so." Row buttons are named for what they act on: "Turn off <label>, version n" and a destructive "Delete version n".

**Action plan and appendix (report).** The plan is a to-do list: the open findings under "Consent platform admin", "Tag manager or marketing", "Site developer" and last "Owner not assigned", each group a numbered list in priority order, one entry per finding with its severity chip, the finding (an in-page link with a visible focus ring), "Ask for:" its first fix step and "Done when: a re-run no longer fails this check.", ending "We recommend re-running the audit after the fixes, to confirm each one." "Not in the plan" lists accepted risks and fixed findings. The plan does not start a page: in print it follows the last finding. The appendix subheads are bordered headings: A Could not check, B Not evaluated, C Left out of the detail, D how the score adds up, E the rules of this audit (fixed column widths, headers never split mid-word), F a legend.

**Findings (report).** Failing findings by severity as cards: badge, area, title, "Failed on 16 of 36 runs where it could be checked, on 1 page, in all 3 choices.", triage (Accepted risk with its reason; "marked fixed by the author, not re-verified"), "What happened" and "What it means for you", a "Fix first (owner)" box and "Next steps" from step 2 for an open finding (a fixed or accepted one says so, has no Fix first box and keeps its steps in Technical detail), "Technical detail" (a disclosure holding "Why it matters", printed open) (semibold labels, about 65 characters wide), one evidence table (Source, Event, Destination, Timing, Consent state; a definition list under 640px, a table from there and in print; Meta and TikTok are "Meta pixel" and "TikTok pixel" without an ID under Off) or "Evidence is not available for this finding in the stored captures.", the undetected-banner note, the owner and the agency's note. The wide tables (consent, evidence, rules) are focusable labelled regions with an edge shadow where more is hidden. The Accept risk dialog says "The reason is printed in client reports."

**Changes** (`/compare`, at the right of the tab bar; the page was called Compare and "comparison" stays as a noun in prose). "Compared with the audit of 14 Oct 2026" and an Audit select (`?against=`) listing only the site's earlier evaluated full audits, with the stored baseline marked; when the shown audit is not the baseline, **Make this the baseline** writes the link and a line says so. Six count buttons (glyph, number, word: Regressed, New, Verdict changed, Fixed, Unchanged, Not comparable; each a filter in `?state=`), then the score line: a delta only for equivalent scope ("Score 72 to 60 (−12)"), otherwise "Score 20 before, 72 now. No change is shown: <why>", and always "Both scores use score version 2, today's arithmetic." One notice lists what limits the comparison (rules versions and how many checks changed, runs shared, banner or consent differences, runs not evaluated, and always that the network location is not recorded). Findings are grouped in that order, one row per change: change badge, severity (or Could not check when the finding never failed), title, a plain line ("Failed on 12 runs before, 12 now. Fails on the same runs."), the triage line ("Marked fixed on 14 Oct, still fails"). Not comparable is collapsed unless filtered. A row opens a side sheet (bottom sheet below `md`, `?finding=`) with the cell table (Page, Choice, Viewport, Before, Now or why not: Not re-checked, Consent choice differed, Cookie banner differed), at most 50 rows ("12 of 24" when cut); focus returns to the row on close. A collapsed **Tags** section (loaded when opened) lists Disappeared, Destination changed, Events changed and New tags as sentences ("TikTok ZINKOTT0001 no longer fires on 6 of 6 runs where it was seen") with a "Show runs" disclosure each, says how many runs were compared and how many were not (a run that did not finish could be missing a tag). **Re-check these pages** in the sheet (and **Re-check this finding** in the finding detail) opens a dialog that states the run count ("3 runs (1 extra run to detect the cookie banner), about 1 minute") and starts a small audit of exactly the cells that changed; a change that rests on one run, flips between audits or belongs to a volatile check carries a **Low confidence** chip and its reasons, and a finished re-check adds one line ("Seen again when re-checked: 2 of 2 runs", "Did not repeat", "Re-check incomplete: 11 of 12 runs could be compared", "Fix confirmed"). A re-check is badged **Re-check** in the site history (its second line names the check) with no score, is titled "Re-check of <check title> · <date>", shows its banner-check and extra runs with a badge and no verdict, and has no Changes or Report content ("Re-checks are not reported"). Its Overview has no score, band or area strip: it leads with the verdict ("No longer fails on the 4 runs re-checked.", "Still fails on 2 of the 4 runs re-checked.", "Re-check incomplete: ...") and says it covers a few runs for one check, then lists only run problems and the check's own worst finding. The finding detail in the audit it re-checked, and in any later audit that failed on the same pages and choices, carries "Re-check, <date>: <verdict>" with a **View the re-check** link; the finding itself keeps its triage status. Empty states: first audit, no baseline, running ("Available when the audit finishes", updated through the audit room), failed, Not evaluated (dashed), a re-check, and "No finding changed in the 12 shared runs."

**What changed (report).** After the summary and before Scope, "What changed since 28 October 2026": the briefing lines, one row per changed finding (glyph and word, severity, title, "On 3 pages of 3 compared", and "may be a flaky result", "did not repeat on re-check" or "re-check incomplete" when that is what the frozen item says), the tag changes, and the notices (rules changed, scope differs, banner or consent differs, scores not compared). It names no page. The consultant's view adds an "Include what changed in the report" checkbox and the list of changes with **Leave out of the report** (also for a Fixed change) and **Show again**; left-out changes are counted ("2 changed findings left out of this report's detail are not listed here") and never named. The register CSV gains "Change since baseline" and "Baseline date".

**Briefing and chips.** An audit with a baseline opens Problems with "Since the audit of 14 Oct 2026" under the score block: up to six lines (the regression first, then new Critical and Warning findings, fixes, tags that no longer fire on any compared page, what could not be compared, and counts with the score and "Both scores use score version 2") and "See all changes". A change that may be a flaky result says "(may be a flaky result; not repeated on re-check)". An audit without a baseline has no briefing; a re-check shows "Re-check of <title>" and its verdict instead. On Findings a row's second line ends with the change (glyph and word, "(low confidence)"), fetched after the list renders and truncated so a row stays two lines at 390px. The site history row has a **Changes** link when the audit has a baseline.

**Findings.** Coverage is a muted line at full coverage and an alert with a segmented bar when partial or mixed. Tabs Open, Accepted risk and Fixed (counts); severity chips and an Area menu, both in the URL. Failing findings are two-line rows under sticky severity headers with a 2px severity rule; "Could not check (n)" is a collapsible group of neutral rows (dashed glyph, the rule's `checkTitle`, the reason, no severity colour). Each failing row ends with "8 of 26 runs" and a thin proportion bar (inline SVG, the number carries the meaning in grayscale). ## Design system

The living version of this section is `/app/design-system` (signed in, not linked from navigation, `noindex`): every token, type role and shared component below, drawn by the real code, with a grayscale preview and fixed sample data. This section names tokens and never copies their values; `styles.css` is the one place with values. When a token, role or shared component changes, change the page and this section in the same change.

### Tokens by role

Use the Tailwind token class (`bg-card`, `text-muted-foreground`, `border-border-subtle`), never a hex or a raw palette colour. Each token has a light and a dark value.

| Role | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Surfaces | `background` (page), `card`, `popover` (also menus, selects, dialogs, sheets), `sidebar`, `muted` (hover and quiet fills), `accent` (selected and current fills), `secondary` |
| Text | `foreground`, `muted-foreground`, and the matching `card-foreground`, `popover-foreground`, `accent-foreground`, `secondary-foreground` |
| Brand and actions | `brand` (the only accent: selection, activity, focus), `primary` and `primary-foreground` (ink, for actions), `destructive` (destructive actions and form errors only) |
| Borders and focus | `border`, `border-subtle` (the hairline of cards), `input`, `ring` (the focus ring) |
| Severity | `sev-critical`, `sev-warning`, `sev-info`, each with `-bg`, `-border`, `-solid` (the glyph fill) |
| Status | `status-pass`, `status-active` (running), `status-neutral` (finished, queued, not audited, unknown), each with `-bg`, `-border`, `-solid` |
| Report | `report-accent`, `report-tint`, `report-on-accent`, set by a `data-accent` attribute from one of eight presets (`lib/report-accent.ts`, mirrored in `styles.css`; each at least 4.5:1 on white). `.report-paper` redeclares the light tokens so a report stays light |

A badge is `border-<set>-border bg-<set>-bg text-<set>`. Tints belong in badges, headers and detail views; rows stay neutral. Could not check is neutral, never amber or green.

### Type roles

- **Display**: the page title (`PageHeader`) and the answer (the score, `text-5xl`). No utility; the size is picked where the answer lives.
- **Section**: `type-section`, applied by `SectionHeading`, `EmptyState` and (one step smaller) `CardTitle`.
- **Label**: `type-label`, applied by `TableHead`, kickers and small group headings.
- **Body**: `text-sm`; long prose `text-base`; meta lines `text-xs`; qualifiers `text-2xs`. `font-mono` for IDs, parameters, URLs and timestamps only.

`type-section` and `type-label` are utilities in `styles.css`; never hand-build either.

### Spacing and radius

Spacing is the Tailwind 4px step. The page frame is `p-4 md:p-6` with `space-y-6` between blocks (`PageShell`); a block's own content uses `space-y-2` to `space-y-4`; rows use `px-3 py-3`; cards `p-4`. Radius steps are `rounded-sm`, `rounded-md`, `rounded-lg` (cards and alerts) and `rounded-xl` (raised surfaces); badges are `rounded-full`. Depth is `shadow-xs` on cards and `shadow-raised` on popovers, menus and dialogs, never a heavier shadow.

### Patterns

- **Page frame and header.** `PageShell` (width `full` for lists and the audit workspace, `readable` for forms) wraps `PageHeader` (the title, a description or meta line, actions at the right). A page does not add its own padding or max width.
- **Tabs and secondary tabs.** Five audit tabs; Evidence is current (underline plus `aria-current="page"`) on Runs, Tags and Hits, which have a second, smaller row under it. Both rows scroll inside their container with soft edges and use the inset focus ring so a clipping ancestor cannot cut it. The active tab is an underline, never a ring.
- **Row.** Hover and keyboard focus fill the row; the ring is drawn around the whole row, inset. Any other control in the row is `relative` so it sits above the stretched link.
- **Notice.** One inline `Alert` per concern, with a title and one line of what to do. A neutral dashed alert means "could not be checked", not a problem.
- **Empty state.** What is missing, what happens next, one action. A first run lists the steps.
- **Needs attention.** On Sites, above the table, absent when empty: at most one row per active site, the first reason that applies (the latest settled audit failed or has incomplete runs; the latest evaluated audit has open Critical findings; it finished more than 24 hours ago, has failing findings and no live share link), each a glyph, a reason and an action (Open runs, Open findings, Open report) on a `DataRow`. Only full audits count. The header meta adds "n need attention".

### Checks before a UI change is done

- **Widths and themes.** Look at 390px and 1440px, in both themes through the real menu (account menu, Theme), with no horizontal page scroll (tables scroll inside their container).
- **Keyboard.** Tab through every new control: the ring is visible, the order follows the reading order, a dialog returns focus to its opener. Targets are at least 32px. Inset rings (`focus-visible:ring-inset`) wherever an ancestor clips overflow: tab bars, table cells, rows, the stage strip.
- **Grayscale.** Anything that relies on status or severity still reads (a glyph and a word). The design system page has a grayscale preview.
- **Vocabulary.** Words come from the table above: Could not check (never "inconclusive"), Not evaluated (never clean), Changes (never "Compare"), Re-audit (never "Verified").
- **Evidence.** Say what you ran and what you saw; "it type-checks" is not "it works".

## Score honesty (batch I)

`components/score-figure.tsx` is the one place that shows a score: Problems when too little was captured (`cell`), Sites, site history (`cell`) and the command palette (`inline`). It takes pure props (`score`, a `ScoreCoverage`, an optional band) so the report can reuse it. `scoreCoverage` in `lib/health-score.ts` sorts a score into two tiers. Tier 1, "Not fully checked" (muted number, partial glyph and words, a no-failures band reading "No failures in what was checked"): run coverage is incomplete, meaning some run has no check results or ended partial or failed (which covers an incomplete audit). It states exact run counts ("1 of 2 runs checked", "2 runs did not finish"). Tier 2: every run was evaluated but some checks could not be resolved. The number keeps full ink and a quiet line says "18 of 19 checks resolved"; a no-failures band reads "No failures in what was checked" with a dashed glyph, never a pass-sounding band. Tier 2 is deliberately quiet because it is near-constant: `hygiene.tag-errors` cannot tell when vendor requests are answered locally, so it is inconclusive on almost every audit, and a label on every audit would mean nothing. Full ink with the plain band and "All runs evaluated, every check resolved" only when every check resolved. The caveats disclosure still lists the unresolved rules. Banner uncertainty is a caveat on findings, not on coverage. The score formula and version are unchanged.

In the Findings coverage line, the per-run counts leave out `hygiene.tag-errors` when it is "could not check" because tracking requests are answered locally (every local audit): otherwise every run would read "could not check" and none "passed". The line says once that endpoint delivery is not checked locally and is left out of those counts; the check is still listed as a Could not check rule and is never counted as passed.

Counts have one definition each. "Could not check" is a rule whose finding has kind `inconclusive`: it could not be checked on any run it flagged and failed on none (a rule that also fails somewhere is a failing finding, not a could-not-check one). The Overview tile, the caveat (first 5 titles, then an "and n more" disclosure), the area strip, the Findings "Could not check (n)" group, the Clients table and the audits list all count that. Check results that could not run (`score.uncertainty.checks`) are a separate unit, named "checks". Findings coverage reads "36 of 40 runs evaluated; 4 did not finish": evaluated means finished with check results, and a partial or failed run is never counted as evaluated even when it has (inconclusive) check results. `ScoreFigure` shows "—" and "Not enough was captured to score" when half the runs or fewer were captured (evaluated and finished), in every size.

Layout: `PageContainer` (`max-w-5xl`, left-aligned) wraps site, New audit and Settings so they share the audit workspace's left edge; the audit workspace is full width. The mobile audit header hides the severity split, duration and "See why" to stay within two lines.

Problems top: the score with "How this is scored" and one collapsed "n caveats" row, never a clean-looking headline over an audit that did not see everything.

## Audit names and score with accepted risks

An audit is named by what it is, in one helper (`auditTitle` in `lib/audit-labels.ts`): "Audit of <site> · <date> · <n> pages" (the header, with the same words minus the date in the compact header and the browser tab, which cannot know the time zone) and "Re-check of <check title> · <date>". The site history shows the date-time with "<n> pages" (or the re-check's check) beneath, and the command palette lists "Audit of <site> · <n> pages" with the date at its right edge.

The headline score counts every failing finding, whatever its triage (see Score honesty: triage is not an input). With accepted risks "How this is scored" says so in one plain sentence under the tiles: "The score of 20 counts all findings, including 1 accepted risk. Without it: 40." The band describes what the headline counts, so it is the worst severity of all failing findings; when every finding at that severity is an accepted risk, the band reads "Critical issues (accepted)" instead of leaving a reader to wonder why a Critical band sits above a single open Warning. The Critical and Warning tiles count open findings only and say "open".

## Published versions and share links

The Report tab's status chip says "Draft, not shared", "Version n published" or "Unpublished changes since version n" (the draft's publication hash against the latest version's), and **Share** opens the share dialog (also at `?share=1`, which the audit header's **Share report** links to once the audit has check results). The dialog: a label only the consultant sees, expiry (7, 30 default, 90 days or never), the sentence "Anyone with the link can read this version without signing in. Focus returns to Share when the dialog closes.

**Export CSV** (Report toolbar and the Findings filter row) downloads the audit register: one row per finding, failing first, then Could not check, with the owner, triage, note and whether the finding is left out of the report.

What a client sees at `/r/<token>`: the report on a plain page (no sidebar, theme menu or app script), a `header` landmark with "Prepared by <agency> · Version n" and a **Save as PDF** button, the client's name as the only h1, and "Version n, published <date>" on the cover; an older version also shows "A newer version was published on <date>" above the report. A link that was turned off says "This report link has been turned off." and one that ran out says "This report link has expired.", each followed by "Ask <agency> for a new link." and nothing else; an unknown or deleted link says "This report was not found." with no hint of why.
